## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies cutting-edge intelligence, efficiency, and seamless human-AI collaboration. The brand projects precision engineering paired with organic fluidity, positioned directly at the intersection of enterprise software and advanced autonomous agents.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Intelligent & Futuristic:** Evokes state-of-the-art conversational AI, smart automation, and next-generation neural architecture without feeling intimidating or cold.
- **Dynamic & Fluid:** Represents constant progression, automated workflows, and frictionless business growth.
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** Instills confidence in business owners, operators, and enterprise executives who entrust their sales pipelines and customer service to autonomous agents.

### Style Archetype: High-Tech Glass & Neo-Emerald Modernism
The design system fuses crisp enterprise ergonomics with radiant emerald lighting, subtle frosted-glass overlays (`backdrop-filter`), and deep forest contrast. Surfaces balance clean clinical light canvases for rapid reading with rich, ultra-deep dark green nodes for focused AI interactions and terminal-grade clarity.

## Colors

The color palette centers on high-luminescence emerald tones anchored by dense, mineral dark green neutrals, completely phasing out previous warm/orange legacy tones in favor of an AI-centric green ecosystem.

### Color Roles
- **Primary (`#00E599` - Electric Mint / Emerald Glow):** Used for focal action points, AI activity indicators, gradient endpoints, interactive badges, and key metrics highlights.
- **Secondary (`#062C23` - Forest Core):** The grounding deep tone. Serves as dark container backgrounds, high-contrast speech bubbles, and dominant branding elements in both light and dark themes.
- **Tertiary (`#22C55E` - Vivid Emerald):** A complementary mid-tier green used to balance readability on light backgrounds, active validation states, and subtle gradient stops.
- **Neutral (`#0B1914` - Dark Slate Black):** Ultra-dark obsidian green utilized for display headlines, heavy text weights, and deep contrast scrims.

### Functional & Surface Shifts
- **Canvas Base:** Crisp clean porcelain `#F8FCFA` layered with translucent green ambient orbs (`rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.08)`).
- **Glass Shading:** Translucent surfaces utilize `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` on light modes and `rgba(6, 44, 35, 0.85)` on dark containers, framed by vibrant green hairline borders (`rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.25)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes clarity, tech-forward geometric proportions, and conversational legibility.

- **Primary Headings & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a friendly yet assertive modern identity with open apertures and clean geometric geometry. Bold weights command authority for value propositions (e.g., "Agente de IA", "Otimize suas tarefas").
- **Technical & UI Labels (`Inter`):** Powers status tags, system notifications, meta-information, and chat timestamps, providing neutral, condensed readability at tiny scales.
- **Text Rhythms:** Generous line heights on paragraph copy maintain effortless scanning across complex conversation trees and system dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt geometric scale, utilizing a flexible 12-column grid on desktop/web applications (collapsing to 4 columns on mobile interfaces).

### Grid & Density
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (`<640px`), Tablet (`640px - 1024px`), Desktop (`>1024px`).
- **Flow & Safe Boundaries:** Outer canvas sections maintain breathing room via `margin` to prevent clutter around hero graphics, floating agent chat pills, and interactive cards.
- **Chat & Component Cadence:** Inter-message conversation threads use `space-sm` (8px), while distinct agent dialogue cards use `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) to demarcate speaker turns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an interplay of frosted glassmorphism, luminous emerald ambient glows, and crisp micro-borders.

### Depth Archetypes
1. **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Subtle off-white base `#F8FCFA` or deep forest slate `#062C23`.
2. **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Dialogue Blocks):** Glassmorphic panels featuring `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with surface fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` (or `rgba(6, 44, 35, 0.75)` in dark containers) bound by a 1px perimeter border of `rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.2)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Agents):** Elevated with soft emerald diffuse shadows: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(6, 44, 35, 0.12), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.15)`.
4. **Level 3 (Neon Focus & AI Indicators):** High-energy radiance using multi-layered glows: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs smooth, rounded geometries that reflect fluid automation and conversational accessibility.

- **Base Components (Cards, Panels, Inputs):** Radius of `0.5rem` (8px) to `1rem` (16px) (`rounded-lg`), producing friendly, ergonomic corners that avoid harsh industrial edges.
- **Feature Cards & Chat Bubbles:** Outer dialogue panels feature generous `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) curves.
- **Pills & Indicator Badges:** Full circular rounding (`9999px`) for metadata pills, tag overlays ("Automação com IA"), status dots, and primary call-to-action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#00E599` fill with `#062C23` bold text. Includes subtle hover expansion and ambient mint glow. Fully rounded pill or `rounded-lg`.
- **Secondary / Contrast:** Deep forest `#062C23` fill with bright `#00E599` or pure white text, bordered by `1px solid rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.3)`.
- **Ghost / Glass:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.35)`, crisp typography with backdrop blur for floating controls.

### Chat & Message Bubbles
- **AI Agent Bubble:** Frosted translucent emerald surface (`rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.15)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` horizontal gradient), bordered with `rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.4)`, dark text, left-aligned with the circular brand icon.
- **User Message Bubble:** Clean forest green background (`#062C23`) with white text and mint accents, right-aligned with avatar framing.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Encapsulated pill structure (`border-radius: 9999px`) with 1px border (`rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.3)`), transparent background, and `Inter` label font in uppercase or title case (e.g., "Automação com IA").

### Cards & Feature Containers
- Framed in smooth `rounded-xl` contours, featuring ultra-subtle gradient borders or soft mint drop shadows.
- Inner divisions leverage hairline rules tinted in `rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.2)`.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- Sleek input containers with `0.75rem` border radius, `#FFFFFF` surface, and `#0B1914` text.
- **Active / Focused State:** Border transitions dynamically to `#00E599` paired with an external `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.2)` ring.
